# Research and evidence

These pages record the comparisons and observations behind releases up to
0.3. They describe the earlier Claim Model, Edition and Candidate design, which
the [product contract](../product-contract.md) replaces; they are history, not
current behavior. Follow their sources and stated limits.

- [Mechanism evaluation](mechanism-evaluation.md): comparisons, ownership and revisit triggers.
- [Source inputs](source-inputs.md): source selection and interpretation mechanisms.
- [Editorial authority](editorial-authority.md): selection, authorship and source boundaries.
- [Renderer mechanisms](renderer-evaluation.md): rendering capabilities and limitations.
- [Composition mechanisms](composition-evaluation.md): layout and complete-figure approaches.
- [Visual acceptance](visual-acceptance.md): independent reading and artwork checks.
- [Dated release-custody evidence](release-custody-readback-2026-09-24.md): historical readback, missing assets and evidence limits.
