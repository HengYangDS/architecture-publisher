# Drawing a view round its centre: three rounds, a committee and a reader

A research page. It records which views a figure drawn round one part tells the truth about, what a prototype drew
and measured in three rounds, what a committee of three seats found in the first round's pictures and a reader in the
second's, and the decision, taken twice by the owner's delegate, that the third round measured as shipped code. It
decides nothing itself: the radial figure enters the product through the `compositions` item's Change, whose design
repeats the decision as Rests on / Considered / Chosen because / Applies to.

## Question

In some views one part is what the others are drawn to: a build graph that stages, renders and checks, a coordinator
that observes, plans and applies. In tiers that part sits in one row and its relations run as long elbows across the
figure, often crossing. Can the publisher draw such a view round that part, from the model, where the drawing tells
the truth and only there?

## Short answer

Yes, for a narrow shape: the part with the most distinct neighbours has three parts or more round it and relates to at
least three quarters of them, no other part relates to more than half of them, and at least twice as many of its
relations go out from it as come in. The rule reads the model alone, so a view is drawn the same way in every
language. On the census it takes three views in both languages, six of 76 plates: their misses of the blank-rectangle
gate fall from 5 to 0 and of all gates from 11 to 3; the pockets between the spokes are the known cost.

## What was read or measured

- The census of dev at bf8e8874: 76 plates of eleven editions, 65 in tiers and 11 matrices, named by its manifests.
- Round 1 (2026-11-27): a prototype drew the census's 31 English views in tiers, of five works, in two free
  compositions, Spine (the longest path as a line of stations) and Radial, each in the box the tiers figure keeps
  beside its reading, and measured them by the four balance gates, crossings and bends; light runs of single plates.
- Three seats (an information designer, a software architect, a book and page designer), agents each alone, read
  round 1's pictures and measures, and the owner's delegate decided on their answers.
- Round 2 (2026-11-27): the first decision as product code, a shape rule and a measured guard, over the census's 76
  plates with dev's code and the prototype's.
- A reader, an agent that had not seen the work, read round 2's pictures of four views in both languages.
- Round 3 (2026-11-28): the revised decision as shipped code, over the same 76 plates.

A spine leaves its corners blank by construction (10 of 13 miss the blank band) and sets one row of every station and

without them was left in tiers.

## The committee (2026-11-27)

All three seats agreed that neither composition is chosen by the blank measure alone, which would pick Radial for
nearly every view, the one form forced on every work that the owner called overuse on 2026-11-02; the choice is by the
view's shape. A radial figure is true where one part is the hub of a star (filmkit `overview`, chosen by all three)
and misleads where its centre is only the busiest part (aigw `authority`, architecture-publisher `models`, proxy
`supervision`, a cycle drawn as a ring). A spine is true only for a pipeline the view's question orders, which the
longest-path test does not tell. No figure may set smaller type than its tiers figure; a view whose author declared
its tiers keeps them; labels stay on their own lines; diagonals read well as a short fan from the centre and worse as
long chords. The seats differed on the centre's share (60% or 75%) and on how to refuse a second hub.

## The first decision and round 2

The owner's delegate took Radial into the next Change and left Spine for a second round. The first rule: the centre
relates to at least 75% of the other parts, no other part to more than half, the author declared no tiers, and a
measured guard (the radial figure passes the blank-rectangle gate, has no more crossings or route defects than its
tiers figure, and no smaller type). Round 2 left 69 of the 76 plates byte for byte as on dev and drew 7 radial, and
showed two faults of the guard: it let a plate trade one gate for another (proxy `failures` in English passed every
gate in tiers and missed holes in radial), and it chose per language, so `projection`, `failures` and
architecture-publisher `page` were radial in one language and tiers in the other.

## The reader (2026-11-27)

The reader judged aigw `projection` and proxy `failures` better radial: every spoke of the coordinator is one of the
steps the question asks about. It judged filmkit `overview` a close call, true on what drives what and weaker on the
order from script to films that its question asks, and architecture-publisher `page` better in tiers, because its
centre is a product that the others write, not a part that drives them. It asked that the choice be made once per
view, from the model, since a reader comparing two editions would take a different drawing for a different structure.

## The decision as revised (2026-11-27, the owner's delegate)

1. Shape, from the model alone: the part with the most distinct neighbours relates to at least 75% of the view's other
   parts, no other part relates to more than half of them, and the centre drives: at least twice as many of its
   relations go out from it as come in (`projection` and `failures` 5 to 0, filmkit `overview` 5 to 2; `page` 3 to 2,
   refused). The author declared no tiers; an author may name `radial`.
2. The choice is the view's, in every language. No measure chooses between tiers and radial: a plate keeps its tiers
   figure only where its radial figure cannot be drawn or would set smaller type, and records why.
3. Labels keep the clearance the route measure requires, 16 px from other lines, one value for every composition, so
   the drawing and its measure agree.

The matrix rule also reads the tiers figure of one language, so a view can be a matrix in one language and tiers in
the other. The same end state applies to it, in a Change of its own.

## What follows for the product, and where it applies

The rule is stated once, beside the matrix rule (`src/poster/compose/plates.mjs`), and reads only the view; the figure
(`radial.mjs`) places its cards on an ellipse in the order whose chords cross least, and draws through the grid's
figure drawing what `free.mjs` sizes and measures. The layout's measure (`src/layout/measure.mjs`) reads a line at any
slant along its own line by the same spacings, so a radial figure and a tiers figure count crossings and route defects
alike, and the clearance a label keeps from other lines is that measure's 16 px. The rule's four thresholds, the ring
of three among them, stand in one constant, `RADIAL_RULE`. The centre is not the view's hub, which the lines draw with
emphasis ([lines](../reference/lines.md)): the hub is the part most relations lead to, the centre counts each
neighbour once whichever way their relations run, and the two may be different parts. It applies to views whose author
declared no tiers, before the matrix rule, which reads the figure in tiers and so may differ between languages. A view
the rule takes whose radial figure cannot be drawn, or would set smaller type than its figure in tiers, is drawn as
the matrix rule or tiers would draw it, and its plate records why radial was refused. An author may name `radial`,
which is drawn wherever it can be and warns where it cannot.

## What is not established

- No person read the pictures; the seats, the reader and the prototype are agents.
- The rounds' measures are light runs of single plates; no edition was built with the shipped code.
- Three plates miss the holes gate as radial figures (`projection` in both languages, `failures` in English), one of
  them newly; reducing the pockets between the spokes is the next round's work.
- The thresholds rest on the six works' views; no other work was drawn.
- A ring of up to ten parts is searched over every order, a longer one by swaps from the view's order. In one light
  run each, the six radial plates took 11.9 s to compose against 11.2 s in tiers on dev, and a made-up view of a
  centre and sixteen parts 17.1 s against 14.5 s on dev, which drew it as a matrix; the host's load differed between
  runs.
- Spine waits for a second round; the matrix rule still chooses per language.
