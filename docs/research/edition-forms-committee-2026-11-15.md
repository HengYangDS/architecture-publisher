# The committee of 2026-11-15 on the edition's forms: a record

A research page. It records what a committee was asked, what its seats answered, what the chair ruled and what the
owner decided. It decides nothing itself, and nothing in it is in the product: each ruling enters through its queue
item's Change, whose design repeats it as Rests on / Considered / Chosen because / Applies to.

## Question

After a hand-set sample book of 37 pages was sent to the owner on 2026-11-15, the owner delegated the open decisions:

> 「你自行做出最佳决策，不确定时，允许召开专家委员会评议决定。我之前给过很多指导，要遵守。注意产品定位、价值和产品力。遵从第一性原理，思考要本质、上位。」

Gloss: "Take the best decision yourself; when unsure, you may convene a committee of experts to deliberate and
decide. I have given much guidance before; keep to it. Mind the product's positioning, value and strength. Follow
first principles; think at the level of essence and of what stands above."

and said of the book's pages of text, in this order:

> 「整页文字的时候，版式是不是还不理想」
>
> 「针对大片文字的情况，整体构图和设计，还不是最理想的状态」
>
> 「针对大片文字的情况，真的适合大屏和投屏演示么」

Glosses: "When a whole page is text, is the layout not yet ideal?" "For large stretches of text, the overall
composition and design are not yet in the most ideal state." "With large stretches of text, is it really suited to
a large screen and to projection?"

The chair put one thing to the committee as standing above every detail of the pages: what the edition is for, under
which viewing conditions, and what belongs on it at all. Five questions followed from it:

| Question | Asked                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| -------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Q1       | What is the edition for, beside the interactive page, the reader file with the data, and the model: who reads each carrier, where, to do what; whether "pages of much text" belong in the edition; one design, two tiers or two outputs |
| Q2       | The reference viewing conditions as distances in image heights and minimum visual angles, for Chinese and for Latin; the type scale they force; what a text page and a plate then hold                                                  |
| Q3       | The page language: the four fields 事实 / 证据 / 未证实 / 下一步 (fact, evidence, not verified, next) on every page: keep, change, or keep for some page classes only                                                                   |
| Q4       | Which page classes enter the product, and in what order                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| Q5       | What makes the product worth choosing over a diagram tool, a documentation generator and an agent that reads the repository itself                                                                                                      |

## Short answer

- The owner ruled above the committee: for people, a room first; a landscape form and a portrait booklet, one source.
- The chair ruled R1 to R10; no ruling was agreed by all seven seats; six of seven took the dissent's one change.
- Every number is a **trial** value until the hand-set proof is read; between two scales the owner preferred B.
- With scale B, by the standard, a far row at 6 H reads the claim and not the statements; the owner's choice stands.
- No page has been read at a distance except by a model, and the Han floor of 20′ is second-hand.

## What the owner decided

Verbatim, in the order received, each with an English gloss made for this page. A gloss is a translation and not the
owner's words.

Recorded at 16:10 CST, one message, sentence by sentence:

> 「图册首先服务于 talks, presentation, reports 等方面，不同协作者之间沟通交流讨论，以及向上汇报，向下教学，最主要优先服务于人类阅读者的场景，其次才是 agents」

Gloss: "The edition first serves talks, presentations and reports, communication and discussion among collaborators,
reporting upward and teaching downward; above all it serves the settings of human readers first, and agents only
after that."

> 「它要把一个事讲明白，并且在受众之间传递信息，达成共识，是沟通交流的工具和载体」

Gloss: "It has to make one matter clear, carry information across an audience and reach agreement; it is a tool and
carrier of communication."

> 「仓库本身不适合做这个事情，而 HTML 又不适合正式演示、talk、出版等场景」

Gloss: "The repository itself is not suited to this, and HTML is not suited to formal presentation, talks,
publication and such settings."

> 「板式 portrait 或者 landscape 均可，landscape 面向 presentation，portrait 面向出版」

Gloss: "The format may be portrait or landscape; landscape is for presentation, portrait for publication."

> 「其实主要是 presentation 场景和电子 booklet 两个场景，我觉得可以补充 portrait 这种情况」

Gloss: "In fact it is mainly two settings, presentation and the electronic booklet; I think the portrait case can be
added."

> 「但优先展示场景，也就是 landscape」

Gloss: "But the display setting comes first, that is, landscape."

> 「而且过多的密集文字，确实不适合投屏，不太适合 landscape；虽然一页都是文字是允许的，但也要有表格，代码块或者其他方面的有效构图设计和组织，不能是大段文字堆砌」

Gloss: "And too much dense text really does not suit projection and does not suit landscape well; a page that is all
text is allowed, but it must have tables, code blocks or other effective composition and organisation; it must not
be long passages of text heaped up."

> 「另外就是文字大小，标题可大，一些解释说明是否字体小，字体大小、样式等，文字排版，构图布局，也可以调研下，最佳实践」

Gloss: "Then there is the size of type: titles may be large; whether some explanations take a smaller size; type

and, with it: 「如果有不明白的，可以再问我」 (gloss: "If anything is unclear, ask me again").

## Findings: the ruling in short

Trial values are marked **trial**: they are numbers the hand-set proof uses and reports on, and by R10 no number
enters the product's value table before that proof has been read. Bracketed names in the working material credit
the seats whose conditions were taken.

| Ruling | In short                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| ------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| R1     | Four carriers of one statement set: presentation edition (landscape 16:9), booklet (portrait), interactive page, reader file with the data. Statements and questions join by stable ids; equal wording across carriers is a check made per language. A presentation page also stands alone when forwarded; the proof tests that on a reader who was not in the room.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| R2     | A presentation page is one claim and one exhibit. Kicker: the question, one line. Claim: a sentence of at most two lines, carrying its own mark: a statement checked against files, or the author's conclusion from the statements under it. Exhibit: a figure, a table, a code block or a list of statements. Beside the count of what the work does not vouch for, the author names at most two such items. No quotation of the pin, no paths, no prose caveats.                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| R3     | What a page shows is the author's, recorded in the model: the claim (optional), the ordered ids of the statements shown, the unvouched items that must be in sight. The build prints that set or fails with the number that fits; it never selects, cuts, shortens or rewords, and there is no continuation page. The draft's R3 is withdrawn. Its first **trial** capacity (five statements of at most two lines, or a table of eight rows) was withdrawn on 2026-11-16: the capacity is six lines of the statement tier, a two-line statement counting as two and a table's rows counting as lines.                                                                                  |
| R4     | The booklet is the complete account, question by question: every statement, its evidence, what is not verified, links onward; the full matrix, the source table, the corrections; silence printed in the question's place. Tables are computed from the data, never typed. The form of the four fields is proofed by hand with the booklet's page. Plates in the booklet use the layout the engine makes as it is; a layout for a portrait body is its own Change.                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| R5     | Viewing conditions are distances, declared as data: a profile, and a parameter of a build. The house's default presentation profile is a reference of 4 image heights. A caller may declare another profile. Refused: gating the profile on the owner stating or measuring a room.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| R6     | A tier is a letter height; each face's size follows. **Trial** tiers in the bundled Han sans (face 0.875 em): 48 / 32 / 24 / 16 pt on the 1600 × 900 page, one pitch of 1.5 em, sans below 48, at most six text styles a page; a second candidate 40 / 28 / 24 / 16. The floor is checked on the letter of each face: a Latin capital in the 24 pt tier is at least 16′ at 4 H, so a face with a 0.66 em capital is set at 26 pt. Nothing is scaled, tracked or condensed to fit.                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| R7     | Plates: fewer labels, not smaller ones. On a presentation plate nothing a room must read stands under the 24 pt tier; a part shows name, kind (once per group) and evidence state; relation labels stay on their lines; a plate labels the relations its claim asserts and draws the others as context without labels; the booklet's plate labels all. Equal labels into one target are said once, byte-equal, carrying every relation's id. The build never splits a view and never shrinks a label. The reading column leaves the presentation plate. What becomes of a view that does not fit was left open, and was ruled on 2026-11-16 after the full run: see the numbers below. |
| R8     | Page classes of the presentation: contents by question on one page, plate, statement page (list, table or code block within capacity), and the part plate once R7's layered plate has been read. Later, each by its Change: the change between two pins, a chronicle strip, a matrix of groups (at most 12 × 12). Course, crossings and whereabouts as figures wait for a second work; until then history is a table of releases dated from tags on a statement page and the full account in the booklet, the future is the `direction` question answered from the pin or printed as silence, and change between pins is `archpub compare`.                                            |
| R9     | Four acceptances, each named: provenance, evidential support, geometry, reading; and links (an outline, linked contents, every cross-reference a link, every statement id a destination; a link across files also prints its target in words and number). The data gains each cited range's text, extracted from the pin. They are checks of the proof's build first and gates of the product only after promotion.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| R10    | Order of work: `fact-layer`, then `reader-and-data`; a proof of three pages by hand, each rendered also as it appears from 4 H and 6 H, with fail conditions set beforehand; the whole second proof; then into the queue, each by its Change: `model-four`, `one-anatomy`, `blocks-by-question`. Breaking rows ship as one release.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |

Numbers the ruling gives beside these:

- R5 rests the default on ANSI/INFOCOMM V202.01 (basic decision making: an element of 2 % of the image's height at
  4 H, 3 % at 6 H) and states that "the tiers of R6" reach: the claim about 9 H, statements 6 H, labels and keys
  4.7 H; fine print is a desk's; so that at the far row of a deep room (6 H) the claim and the statements are read
  and the labels are not. Those reaches are scale A's: the chair said so in the ruling on 2026-11-16 and added the
  reaches of scale B, which follow. The booklet declares its fit on a screen, and its body holds the floors there
  without zoom.
- The reaches of scale B, the owner's choice, by the same reading of the standard: an element's height is 0.5 % of
  the image's height for each image height of distance, so a letter of h pt on the 900 pt page reaches h / 4.5 image
  heights; with the Han face's 0.875 em the claim reaches 7.8 H (40 pt em, 35 pt letter), statements 5.4 H (28 pt em,
  24.5 pt letter), labels and keys 4.7 H (24 pt em, 21 pt letter), fine print 3.1 H. So at the default profile of
  4 H every room tier is read, and at the far row of a deep room (6 H) the claim is read and the statements and
  labels are not, by the standard. R5's sentence that statements are read at 6 H does not hold for scale B. The
  ruling says that the owner chose B on the pages and their renderings at 4 H and 6 H, that the choice stands, and
  that this consequence is said to the owner with the proof.
- R6 as decided by the owner: scale B, 40 / 28 / 24 / 16, is the proof's scale; capacity follows from it: six lines
  of the statement tier on a statement page, a two-line statement counting as two and, by the chair's paragraph of
  2026-11-16, a table's rows counting as lines.
- R7: the question of a view that does not fit waited for a run of the works' 41 views in both languages at the trial
  sizes, reporting wiring beside fit, and for one hand-set layered plate; on the one work measured at the time, five
  of six views fit at 32 and 24 pt, tightly, and four wired worse.
- R7's open part, ruled by the chair on 2026-11-16 after that run ended, under the same delegation: a view's
  presentation is one base and one plate for each claim the author records for it; every plate stands on that base,
  with the same parts in the same places, the claim's relations inked and labelled at the 24 pt tier and the others
  drawn without labels. Where the base does not fit with names at the room tier, or a claim's labels do not fit on
  it, the build refuses that view's plate by name, for the language concerned, and says what did not fit and by how
  much; the author answers in the model, and the build chooses nothing. It rests on the
  [room-size fit](room-size-plate-fit.md) (with names at 32 pt and every label at the 24 pt tier, 17 of 41 views are
  placed in Chinese and 7 in English; with every label at 16 pt, 34 and 26; at 36 and 27 pt, 5 and 1) and on the
  hand-set plates of [the second proof](second-proof.md). The owner can turn it.
- R10's fail conditions: a reader of another origin, given the 4 H rendering, misreads or cannot read any room-tier
  text; given the 6 H rendering, the claim or a statement; given one page alone, cannot state its point and what the
  work does not vouch for; the tidiness measure reports a finding; an authored set does not fit. The renderings are
  the page at 45 % and 30 % of a 27-inch screen at 60 cm.

## The dissent

Written before the chair ruled, on the draft, by a reviewer of another origin. Ruling by ruling, in short:

| On  | The dissent                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| --- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| R1  | Wording is a fragile identity: rule stable question and statement ids, with explicit audience, selection and order per telling. The owner's choice of B settles depths and leaves audience-specific tellings unresolved.                                                                                                                           |
| R2  | A count cannot disclose which uncertainty changes the decision. The layout study supports assertion and evidence for narrated talks and does not establish silent reading. Rule essential qualifications beside the claim and a linked deeper account. It adds that R3's question-only fallback fails R2's requirement of a headline that answers. |
| R3  | "No build selection" and "the first that fit" contradict each other. Rule presentation selections, omissions and essential qualifications in `model-four`, approved by the author; paginate or refuse overflow without silently changing the selection.                                                                                            |
| R4  | The four fields become a compulsory booklet grammar without comparison. Rule required information, composed for each question.                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| R5  | Neither 4 H nor 6 H is an observed condition of the owner; the study distinguishes conflicting standards and content categories. Rule a declared viewing profile and task.                                                                                                                                                                         |
| R6  | The draft's own arithmetic gives Latin sans 15.1′, below R5's 16′; at 6 H that label is about 10.1′. Four sizes and six styles are design choices, not demonstrated perceptual limits. Rule glyph-based compliance per face and viewing profile.                                                                                                   |
| R7  | Not yet an implementable contract. Engineering's assumed 4 to 7 fitting views concern 21.3 pt, not 24 pt. Compare fixed-base layers, author splits and explicit refusal, recording retained relations and author effort.                                                                                                                           |
| R8  | Deferral leaves its coverage consequence unstated. Rule an explicit current carrier for history, the present and plans, distinguishing recorded plans from accomplished facts.                                                                                                                                                                     |
| R9  | Byte and predicate equality and tidiness measures check projections; they do not establish citation support or comprehension. Rule separate acceptance for provenance, evidential support, geometry and reading.                                                                                                                                   |
| R10 | Merely seeing a proof is undefined acceptance. Rule a compact comparative proof first, then the complete book, with acceptance criteria and migration scope before promoting any prescription.                                                                                                                                                     |

Each point names what would withdraw it: a comparison, a reading or a run that shows the draft's rule holds. The
dissent also quotes one sentence of the owner that is not among those above, on carriers for history, the present
and plans; it is not repeated here, because this page saw it only as the dissent quotes it.

Its "one change before ruling": record author-approved presentation selections and essential qualifications in
`model-four` before setting capacity, because "Mechanical fidelity cannot rescue misleading selection." Six of seven
seats took it; R3 as amended carries it.

Silence it says requires settlement: PDF reading order and accessibility; internal against cross-file destinations;
localization and changed wording; omission authority; overflow recovery; preservation of published editions; and
acceptance of the screen booklet's geometry.

What it could not check, by its own account: product code, current releases, PDFs, redesigns, fonts, the tidiness
implementation, the source studies themselves, and actual viewers or readers; it was given papers, not those
artifacts.
