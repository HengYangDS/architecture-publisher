# Narrow notes: a committee's record

A research page. It records what a committee was asked about the setting of a Chinese note in a narrow column of the
overview, what its three seats answered in two rounds, what was measured and drawn for them, and what the owner
said before and after. It decides nothing itself: the rule enters the product through the Change
`justify-a-narrow-note-to-its-own-measure`, whose design repeats it as Rests on / Considered / Chosen because /
Applies to.

## Question

Under a station of a course and in the cells of the blocks below, the overview sets short notes of one to four
lines in columns 88 to 168 pt wide, seven to fifteen Chinese characters a line. Many hold Latin words that cannot
break. Which rule should the product use for a Chinese note of more than one line there, that it can apply without
a list of words and without anyone placing or rewording anything?

## Short answer

The note keeps its breaks and is justified to its own longest line, not to its column: a shorter line but the last
is spread to that line where it opens a tenth of an em or less at each place, and is set as it is otherwise. No
line before the last is left to a single character where the same number of lines holds the note otherwise. No
author is asked to reword a note so that its lines fill. Text in a measure of twenty ems or more stays justified
to its column. All three seats chose this, each alone and again after reading one another, and the owner took it
after seeing the comparison.

## Findings: where the three seats agree

The seats: a typographer and editorial designer of Chinese books and magazines; a designer of talks, slides and
teaching diagrams; a keeper of the owner's constraints, who holds each option against his words and has no taste
of its own in the matter.

1. **The note's own longest line is its measure.** Spread to the column's edge, first lines such as 由提供商提供的模型，名为
   and 带可信的 SSH stand visibly opened above lines at their natural spacing; at their own measure they read at one
   pitch. On a whole page the stations, their names and the notes' common left starts hold a row together, and the
   uneven right ends do not break it.
2. **A tenth of an em at each place, and no further.** A line that would open more is set as it is, flush left.
   Spreading every line to the note's longest however far opens two lines of a four-line note again.
3. **The breaks of before, with no weight for a punctuation mark.** A caption's even breaks improve some notes
   (具体到路径， / 写入前一刻) and split words in others (拉 / 出); without a list of words either rule splits some.
4. **No single character alone on a line before the last**, where the same number of lines holds the note
   otherwise; a whole Latin word on a line of its own is legitimate; a line is never added to avoid it.
5. **No author is asked to reword for fit.** Twelve rewordings made that day so that lines fill were faulted five
   times by a second reader. Text that overflows is still refused.
6. **A measure of twenty ems or more keeps the rule of before.**
7. **The choice strains the owner's words.** He asked five times for two aligned edges, once of Chinese details by
   name; his allowance of flush left was for English. With this setting notes side by side end at different right
   edges, and some lines end short within a note. Each seat said so and asked that he be shown whole pages.

## Where the seats differed

On the warning `overview_line_loose`. In the first round two seats dropped it as a warning to an author and one
kept it as a diagnostic that names the note, the line and how far it ends short and asks for no rewording. In the
second round that seat joined the two; one of the two offered to sign the diagnostic instead. No seat refused
dropping it for a narrow note. On when the product should change: one seat, before the owner had seen whole pages;
two, after the committee agreed, under his delegation. He saw them before any change.

## What follows, and where it applies

- The setting, the single character, the warning and the measure of twenty ems: the Change
  `justify-a-narrow-note-to-its-own-measure` (queue item `overview`).
- The works' statements keep their authors' wording: the publications, with the release that holds that Change.

## Not established

- That a tenth of an em is the right bound for other faces and sizes: judged on this house's faces at 11 and
  12.5 pt.
- What a room sees on a projected page: every seat judged pictures on a screen, and every seat was an agent, as
  every reader of this product so far.
