## Why

The semantic compiler detects changes beyond refresh, but candidate preparation
still hardcodes one node and three page edits. This leaves source evolution
dependent on product-code edits instead of explicit, reviewable authored input.

## What Changes

- Replace the refresh-only preparation contract with source-bound view edits.
- Preserve complete views, stable identities, explicit reading order and existing
  geometry checks; reject unsupported or unrepresented changes.
- Bind static visible carriers and interactive edits to exact source values.
- Qualify one real committed non-refresh ETHOS change through both media and
  recoverable replacement, recording editing and verification costs.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

- `ethos-atlas`: explicit source-bound authored candidate edits beyond refresh.
- `static-publishing`: checked semantic carrier edits in an existing composition.

## Impact

Extend existing edition, composition and qualification owners. No ETHOS source
mutation, universal solver, new runtime dependency, parallel authority or
replacement of accepted reader files. This is E1 of the approved A-E tranche;
later general layout, second-adopter and ecosystem stages remain out of scope.
