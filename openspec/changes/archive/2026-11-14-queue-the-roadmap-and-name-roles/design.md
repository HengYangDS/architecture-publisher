## Goals / Non-Goals

**Goals.** The owner: 「先形成一个比较完善的产品形态」 (first form a fairly complete product form) (dossier). "Complete" is defined
[product]: by step 8, on at least one work, six things are closed.

1. The contents of every projection is the same question table.
2. Every entry is in one of the three states.
3. The author and the reviewer have each passed one replacement test.
4. The reader check has a result.
5. Pin lag is reported routinely.
6. One third-party work has gone through the whole path.

**Non-Goals** (through step 8).

- New palettes, styles, compositions and typefaces; a revision and annotation layer; decoration of the round seal
  [all, critic].
- A binding language that authors can write; a general grammar-of-graphics engine [all].
- New distribution channels, platforms and languages. zh-TW, es and ko are marked in the contract as not tested by a
  work. A release is cut only when a step completes.
- Genres, three levels of depth, a graph, the fifteen-repository projection; an MCP service and an API.
- No more tuning of the balance gate, automatic composition and automatic type-size tiers; the last is deleted in
  step 5b [typography]. Cost: the 63 plates that fail the balance gate do not improve before step 5.
- The existing selection layer on top of Archify (1,040 lines) is frozen; LikeC4 import gets no new work
  [engineering].
- Before step 4 is accepted, no new retrospective and no new committee; "open-source hit" is no near-term goal
  [critic, documentation, product].

## Decisions

The subsections from "Content" through "Where each constraint is carried" are candidate designs. They are
unverified, and the results of step 1 can overturn all of them [critic]. The owner on 11-03: 「一定要切实验证想法再提」 (be sure
to verify an idea properly before proposing it) (dossier).

### Essence and positioning

**Derivation.** The owner: 「承认系统/流程/约束/原则的客观性，但要求实践者/实施者是可以置换的」 (accept that systems, processes, constraints and
principles are objective, and require that practitioners and implementers be replaceable) (dossier). Holders can be
replaced only if the objective layer is written outside every holder [all]. Code is already outside the holder, and
it answers only "what is there now". Constraints, processes, decisions, direction and "what is absent here" are
missing from code or cannot be read from it reliably [product, documentation, critic, agent]. Every agent session is
one cold-start replacement [product, engineering, critic]. For written words to be trusted in use, they must be set
against a commit, each sentence must trace to evidence, a role must answer for them, and staleness must be
computable [product, engineering, typography].

Conclusion [all]: archpub fixes the objective layer of a system at one commit as a set of statements with evidence,
and does three things [agent]. It **checks**: statements and evidence against the commit, and the difference against
a second commit. It **projects**: the same statements into forms each class of reader can read. It **records**:
which role's holder did what to which bytes. The edition and the web page are projections of that body.

### Content: three states, evidence, where content lives

| Topic                                         | Decision                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| --------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Three states                                  | [all; predicate from language and documentation] A question-table entry q is "answered" on a work if and only if at least one question marks q and at least one statement it selects has evidence. For components and relations the cited files must exist at the pin. For text statements a line range is required, it must exist at the pin, and its digest is recorded. Otherwise the author must write "not applicable" with a reason, or "silent" with the paths and patterns searched. If none is written, `check` refuses. If an exception is written and the entry also meets "answered", `check` refuses too. A silence search can be replayed; a replay that finds a hit is refused [language].                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| Wording and level of verification             | Wording does not exceed the level of verification [documentation]. Mechanical verification reaches only "the cited line range exists", and that cell reads "cited". It reads "supported" only after a reviewer has judged, item by item, that the evidence supports the sentence. Support is a judgement in a record and is not merged with strength into one number [language].                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| The question table belongs to publications    | [all] It is a data file, and each row names the role that asks [documentation]. The owner on 11-07: 「都要兼顾，新人上手和评审决策优先」 (cover them all; newcomers getting started and evaluation for decisions come first) (dossier). Newcomers and evaluators are two kinds of reader. archpub knows only the three states [engineering]. The initial content is ruling D8.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| It does not turn into form-filling            | [product, documentation, engineering, language] The check looks at evidence and ignores word count. Pages are titled with the work's own questions. Operational entries (getting started, verification and release) are answered by a pointer that cites the place in the described repository where it is written [documentation].                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| Relation to the 11-07 words                   | The owner said a work should contain 「顶层设计，机制讲解，路线图，技术栈」 (top-level design, explanation of mechanisms, roadmap, technology stack) 「还有仓库结构」 (and the repository structure) (dossier). The three states make sure every entry must be faced and allow the answer to be silence. Default: silence is printed as is. An author statement with no evidence in the repository is printed with the fixed mark "unevidenced" and does not count as an answer. | Evidence                                      | There is one evidence table; `files`, `@source` and the source table all point to it [agent, documentation, language]. Strength has four levels. The levels: none; the file or line range exists; the cited content contains the other side's path or module name (defined for relations only); enforced by a test or gate. Acceptance: delete the explainer script, and the reader file and the edition are still complete.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |

### How the figure half is checked during building

Several members wrote down the same doubt. Figures take two thirds of `src`, their main audience is people, and

By ruling 6 the checking of figures by agents during building rests on the owner's own words. It is more than the

can read them, and whether it misreads them, is measured and recorded during building [all, round 3].

Three questions are left to the owner. Each has a default that is carried out if he says nothing.

  owner raises a new direction, the chair states on the spot what it displaces, and if he says jump the queue, it
  jumps.
