## Context

See [proposal.md](proposal.md). The queue and the design behind it were written on 2026-11-14 by a committee whose
conclusion was that the product "fixes the objective layer of a system at one commit as a set of statements with
evidence", with the edition and the page as projections of that set
(`openspec/changes/archive/2026-11-14-queue-the-roadmap-and-name-roles/design.md`, "Essence and positioning"). The
sample editions were set from that sentence. A third proof, set after the owner's words, is the same work as
figures: 16 of its 18 pages are figures, and its contents page says for each of the 27 questions which figure
answers it (22), which table (1), which has no figure yet (1) and which the work does not answer (3). It is the
maintainer's local material and was sent to the owner.

## Goals / Non-Goals

**Goals:**

- The queue's remaining items say what the product builds next under its own positioning.
- A reader of the research pages can tell which premises were withdrawn and where the present state stands.

**Non-Goals:**

- Any change of the product's source, of a delivery or of the model's format: each restated item lands by its own
  Change.
- Rewriting the design of 2026-11-14 or the committee's record of 2026-11-15: both stand as records.
- Deciding the visual style in which a work's figures of different kinds agree; the sample book's next proof shows
  it first.

## Decisions

1. A work is its figures, and the queue's items are read so.
   - Rests on: the contract's Product section; the owner's words of 2026-11-16 quoted in the proposal; the counts
     of the three proofs; the archived design's queue rule that the owner's feedback enters as one commit and its
     stop line for the sample edition.
   - Considered: keeping the items and improving the pages of statements (done for two days: larger type, tables,
     a tidiness measure, a committee on their form); dropping the question table.
   - Chosen because: the owner decided the positioning, and the contract had it. Statements with evidence are what
     makes a figure trustworthy, not what a reader is shown in its place. The question table stays as the check of
     what a work covers and as the contents that lead to figures: covering more of a system means more kinds of
     figures.
   - Applies to: `sample-book`, `model-four`, `one-anatomy`, `figure-pages`, and every later item's reading.
2. The statements, their line ranges and the strength and origin of their evidence enter the model as the notes of
   a figure's parts and lines, and a figure carries its claims.
   - Rests on: the third proof, where each page is one figure under one sentence of the work, and the 22 records
     in which a holder acting as the author gave a question its claim in one to three minutes
     (`docs/research/second-proof.md`).
   - Considered: statements as a list of their own beside the views (the item as first written); no statements in
     the model.
   - Chosen because: a statement that belongs to no figure has no place on a page of this product, and one that
     belongs to a part or a line is found where a reader looks. The data and the reader file still hold all of
     them.
   - Applies to: `model-four`.
3. Type sizes are derived from a declared viewing condition and a table of least visual angles; what does not fit
   loses detail, never size; a figure states its reach.
   - Rests on: `docs/research/reach-of-type-on-figures.md`: the faces' measured letter heights, the reach of the 82
     plates the product makes today, and two views built under two conditions.
   - Considered: one fixed size for a room (24 pt, ruled on 2026-11-15 and withdrawn); two fixed scenes, a desk and
     a projection.
   - Chosen because: a typed size hides its premise, a distance and an angle, and the product cannot know the room.
     This is an intent of the item; its own Change shows it on more views before it rules, and the angles need a
     person's eye.
   - Applies to: `one-anatomy`.
4. `blocks-by-question` is renamed `figure-pages`.
   - Rests on: decision 1: the edition is set figure by figure, and the questions are its contents.
   - Considered: keeping the name.
   - Chosen because: an item's name says what it builds. Records of earlier rulings keep the name they used.
   - Applies to: the contract's queue and the research pages that point forward to the item.

## Risks / Trade-offs

- [The owner has not answered on the third proof] → The archived design says no step waits for him; the restated
  items hold only what his words of 2026-11-16 decide, and what is still a proposal (decision 3, the style) is
  marked as one.
- [Two days of work on pages of statements is set aside] → Its findings stay in the research pages with a note of
  what was withdrawn; the claims the authors recorded become the figures' claims.
