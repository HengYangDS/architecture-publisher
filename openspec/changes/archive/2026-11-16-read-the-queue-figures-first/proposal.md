## Why

The contract says what the product is: it draws the architecture of a repository from an explicit model, a plate for
each view. The queue's items `sample-book`, `model-four` and `blocks-by-question` led away from that: "a work covers
more than structure" was carried out as 27 questions answered by written statements, and the two hand-set sample
editions of 2026-11-15 and 2026-11-16 came out as pages of text (2 figures in the 30 pages of the presentation
edition, none in the 189 pages of the booklet). The owner, on seeing them on 2026-11-16: 「产品定位应该是架构图，以图
为主，不是堆砌文字吧？」「文字的部分只是辅助？」「整个产品定位本来就应该是以图为主的」. The design behind the queue
names this case: the stop line of the sample edition is that the owner rejects its form, and his feedback enters as
one commit to the queue, after which the later items are sorted again.

## What Changes

- The queue states above its items how they are read: a work is its figures, and what the product writes (a
  figure's claim, the notes of its parts and lines, the reader file, the data) assists them.
- `sample-book` is restated as one work set by hand figures first, and says what the owner's first look decided.
- `model-four` gives the model what a figure needs: each view's claims and the relations each asserts, the guide's
  diagrams as figures of the edition, the questions a figure answers, and statements with their evidence as the
  notes of a figure's parts and lines.
- `one-anatomy` also takes type sizes derived from a declared viewing condition, levels of detail in place of
  steps of smaller type, and each figure's stated reach.
- `blocks-by-question` becomes `figure-pages`: the figure page, the contents by question and the table of terms.
- `docs/roadmap.md` records how the steps after the sample edition were sorted again, and the research pages say
  which of their premises were withdrawn; one new page records the reach of type on figures.
