## Why

On 2026-11-21 the owner consented to publishing the works in public and said that they will grow, and that what is
built for them is to be designed for that. The contract's last item, `gallery`, says only that the published works
are shown in public once the product's form is whole: nothing in it keeps a gallery from being a page made by hand
for the six works there are today.

## What Changes

- The item `gallery` says that the gallery is made by the publisher's own command from the works a publications
  repository declares, so that a new work, a new language or a new medium appears in it with nothing listed or
  placed by hand, and records the owner's consent and his word that the works will grow.

The queue's order is unchanged: `gallery` stays last.
