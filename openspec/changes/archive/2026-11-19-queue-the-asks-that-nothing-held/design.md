## Context

See [proposal.md](proposal.md). The inventory behind it, with each ask, the owner's words and what was found in the
product, is kept with the maintainer's working records and is not a tracked file; this Change puts its result
where the product's work is ordered.

## Goals / Non-Goals

**Goals:**

- Every ask of the product that is not done is named by an item of the queue.
- The repository holds the two community files the owner asked for.

**Non-Goals:**

- Building any of the items: each is a Change of its own, in the queue's order.
- The asks that belong to the works and not to the product: a palette for each work, the Chinese byline and two
  measurements made at their pins are a Change of the publications.

## Decisions

1. The three dropped asks stand before `performance`.
   - `performance` is open and ends by a measured condition; an item behind it would wait for that. Each of the
     three was asked before `performance` joined the list, and two are faults against the contract as it reads
     today: no input places anything, and a delivery is for its readers.
   - What moves back: `performance` and every item after it, by three. The Change in flight for `performance`
     lands before this one.
2. What an existing item left out is added to that item and makes no item of its own.
   - `performance`: the two media of a build made side by side. `edition-map`: every view reached from the
     overview, the contents on one page, a narrow note broken between words. `compositions`: the untidiness a
     census finds and no gate holds. `portrait-edition`: the name "print edition" retired, which stands in 98
     places of the documents, the source, the tests and the specs and is therefore no maintenance.
     `page`: an overview, and the model's question as its title. `carriers`: the technology a work stands on,
     coverage by the kind of repository, and composed pages of text. `palettes-and-styles`: more built-in
     palettes and the owner's own style.
3. The code of conduct adopts the Contributor Covenant 3.0 by reference.
   - Considered: the project's own four sentences, which `CONTRIBUTING.md` held. The owner asked for the practice
     of the field; the Covenant is that practice, and a link keeps its text its publisher's.
   - The file says where the code holds here and how to report, which the Covenant leaves to each project.
4. The security file names examples only of what the contract itself promises.
   - A build changes no checkout and needs no network; a publication never replaces a different delivery;
     `archpub verify` holds a delivery to its manifest. A way around one of these is a vulnerability.
   - Reports go to the maintainer privately by the address on the public profile: the Forge's private reporting
     is not enabled for the repository, and enabling it is its owner's setting.
5. The README's plate is built from the publisher's own work as the publications hold it, by the released 0.15.0.

## Risks / Trade-offs

- [The queue grows from eleven open items to fifteen] → each new item is one bounded ask; none is a wish of the
  maintainer's.
- [An item worded with more than it held grows without end] → an addition names one observable thing, and the
  Change of an item still states its own requirements.
