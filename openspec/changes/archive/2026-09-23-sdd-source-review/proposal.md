# Proposal

## Why

The current mechanism evaluation covers the initial SDD comparison but omits
three adjacent implementations with concrete relevance to ETHOS intent context,
agent governance, and cross-runtime guidance. A source-level supplement is
needed before recommending adoption or rejection from product descriptions.

## What Changes

- Add an exact-revision review of SpecDD, AgentSpec, and SpecRoute to the
  existing research record.
- Separate implemented mechanisms from marketing claims and identify bounded
  adopt, reject, or defer decisions at the ETHOS and Publisher boundaries.
- Record inspection limits and reassessment triggers without adding a second
  intent, workflow, or quality authority.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

None.

### Modified Capabilities

None. This is a research-only documentation update with no product behavior change.

## Impact

Only `docs/research/mechanism-evaluation.md` and this Change are affected.
Packages, APIs, canonical specifications, release artifacts, and signed tags
remain unchanged.
