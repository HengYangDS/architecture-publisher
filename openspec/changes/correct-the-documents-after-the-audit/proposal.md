## Why

On 2026-11-16 the owner asked for an audit with independent reviewers. One reviewer checked the facts and numbers
of the research pages, the contract, the changelog and the README against their sources: of 184 claims listed, 179
were checked, 157 were right, 16 were wrong and 6 could not be traced. Another compared every place where a record
says what the owner said or decided with a ledger of the owner's own messages. The research pages on `dev` hold
wrong numbers and wrong times that those reviewers named, they call the owner's decision what was the owner's pick
of an option the builder had worded, or a ruling of the committee's chair, and six of them report rulings that were
withdrawn on that day without saying so. The README and the tutorial say that a first work takes "about fifteen
minutes", which nobody timed.

## What Changes

- The nine pages of `docs/research/` are corrected: the facts and times the reviewers found wrong, and each place
  that says what the owner said, picked or preferred, by the ledger's words and times. A ruling of the committee of
  2026-11-15 is named as its chair's.
- A number that was re-derived says from what, and a number whose source could not be traced says so.
- Six pages gain a dated note under their title: which of the rulings they report were withdrawn on 2026-11-16, by
  which of the owner's words, and that the page stands as a record of what was read and measured.
- The index of `docs/research/` says that the two sentences it quotes were said to another session about another
  product, and names the decisions that exist, D1 to D7.
- `README.md` and `docs/tutorial.md` no longer say how long a first work takes.
