## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Panels beside the conclusion

A page of two to four views SHALL list beside its conclusion each panel's
title, numbered as the panels are and linked to its panel. A page of more views
SHALL list none, because the list is the conclusion's reasons and Archify
holds at most four; its panels stay numbered in the plan's order.

#### Scenario: The panels beside the conclusion

- **WHEN** a model declares three views
- **THEN** the conclusion is followed by the three panels' titles, numbered 1
  to 3, each a link to its panel

#### Scenario: More panels than the conclusion holds

- **WHEN** a model declares five views
- **THEN** the conclusion stands alone
- **AND** the page has five numbered panels in that order
