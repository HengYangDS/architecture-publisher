## Why

The contract's first sentence says what the product is: it draws the architecture of a repository from an explicit
model. The queue that the Change `queue-the-roadmap-and-name-roles` set on 2026-11-14 led away from it. Its design
took the product for "a set of statements with evidence" and forbade new compositions and styles until the queue's
last item. Under it two sample editions were set by hand as pages of statements, and a third carried the page's
Archify diagrams into the PDF; the owner objected to each on seeing it. On 2026-11-16 the owner asked for an audit
with independent reviewers. Read against every sentence the owner had typed since 2026-10-26, the queue's open
items held none of what the owner had asked of the figures themselves, and its standing rule forbade most of it:
a part whose card and strip compose (asked on 2026-11-14, 2026-11-15 and 2026-11-16), a portrait edition beside
the landscape one (2026-11-15 and 2026-11-16), several compositions chosen by a view's shape (approved on
2026-11-11), and carriers for history, plans and change (2026-11-15).

## What Changes

- The contract's Product section says what the edition and the page are each for, and that each medium draws its
  own figures.
- The Boundaries no longer say that slides are not produced: the edition's landscape pages are made to be shown as
  slides.
- The queue keeps its six finished items and orders the open ones by the dimensions of the edition's figure
  language: `composable-parts`, `portrait-edition`, `compositions`, `carriers`, `palettes-and-styles`, `page`,
  `roles-and-records`, `foreign-work`.
- The items `sample-book`, `model-four`, `blocks-by-question`, `six-works` and `freeze-and-foreign-work` leave the
  queue, and with them the rule that the product takes no new palette, style or composition until the last item.
- An item is accepted on the published works as the product builds them, with what its own gates record.
- The queue's finished items no longer narrate the owner's remarks, and `docs/roadmap.md` says which of its steps
  no longer direct work.
