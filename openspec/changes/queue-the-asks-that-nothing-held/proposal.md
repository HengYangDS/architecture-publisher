## Why

On 2026-11-19 the owner read the six works as 0.15.0 built them and found asks of his that nothing held: a figure
alone on a transparent ground, which the maintainer had promised on 2026-11-17 and never listed; positions written
by hand in the page's diagrams, where the contract says no input places anything; a reader file that no reader at
a screen had been shown; and more. He asked for all of them to be found and none to be dropped. Readers other than
the maintainer went through everything he had said since 2026-10-26 against the product: twenty-four asks had no
holder, neither a gate nor an item of the queue.

The queue is where an ask of the product waits. An ask that is not in it is forgotten when the next subject
comes, which is how these were lost.

## What Changes

- The queue takes three items before `performance`, each an ask that was dropped: `figures-alone`, `page-figures`
  and `reader-file`. `gallery` joins at its end.
- Seven items name what they had left out: `performance`, `edition-map`, `compositions`, `portrait-edition`,
  `page`, `carriers` and `palettes-and-styles`.
- The contract says which two readers a work's coverage is chosen for.
- `SECURITY.md` and `CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md` stand at the root, as the owner asked on 2026-11-12 and the repository
  paradigm now admits; `CONTRIBUTING.md` points to them, and says which channels a release has to reach: the
  release of 0.15.0 left the Homebrew formula at 0.14.0 for half a day.
- The tutorial takes the document role `tutorial`, which the paradigm has since named.
- The plate in the README is the one 0.15.0 builds; it was built by 0.13.1.
