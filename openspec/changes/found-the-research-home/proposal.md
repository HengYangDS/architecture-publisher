## Why

The rulings of 2026-11-15 on the edition's forms (a presentation edition and a booklet, tiers by letter height,
tidiness as a measured property, links in the PDFs) rest on two studies, a calibrated measure, a committee's record
and two hand-set proofs. All of it lies in `build/runtime/work/`, an ignored directory of one checkout: a design that
says "Rests on" has nothing in the repository to name. The owner's direction of that day, relayed to this
repository: 「说简单点，会话易失、仓库稳定，关键的东西需要沉淀到仓库中最佳位置和载体上」「另外，docs/research
也要好好建设下，调研很重要」.

## What Changes

- `docs/research/` holds an index with the directory's rule and eight pages: letter height by viewing distance,
  text on projected pages, the layout of pages that are mostly text, tidiness built in and measured, links and
  outlines in the PDFs Typst writes, the record of the committee of 2026-11-15, the room-size fit of the works'
  views, and what the second proof showed.
- A research page records and decides nothing: it has one form (question, short answer, what was read or measured,
  findings, what was compared, what follows and where it applies, what is not established, record), and a decision
  in a Change's design names the page it rests on.
- `docs/README.md` routes to the index, and `CHANGELOG.md` records the directory.
